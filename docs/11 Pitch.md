Primary Research Question

    "How can an integrated, multi-tenant SaaS architecture that combines physical asset lifecycle management with visual data pipeline (ETL) workflows overcome the high costs, data sovereignty issues, and system fragmentation prevalent in current cloud inventory solutions?"

Supporting Sub-Questions (Aligned with your framework)

Phase 1: The Broad Context (The Consensus)

    Sub-Question 1: How do standard supply chain methodologies and traditional multi-tenant SaaS architectures currently approach high-volume inventory tracking and data processing?

Phase 2: The Specific Issues (Divergent Ideas)

    Sub-Question 2: What are the primary technical and operational limitations of existing siloed inventory applications (such as Sortly or Odoo) and decoupled workflow automation tools (such as Zapier or n8n) , particularly regarding escalating costs, vendor lock-in, and data sovereignty?

    Sub-Question 3: What are the inherent scalability and system efficiency challenges when utilizing a PostgreSQL database with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) for high-volume concurrent inventory updates?

Phase 3: The Gap and Your Contribution (The Focus)

    Sub-Question 4: To what extent does unifying barcode-driven inventory tracking (e.g., the StoQR module) and a drag-and-drop workflow editor into a single workspace improve operational efficiency and data-informed decision-making compared to fragmented third-party systems?
