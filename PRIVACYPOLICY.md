# OpenSe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-24

OpenSe is provided by Rcsn01. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled
for self-hosted OpenSe instances and for any trial, beta instance hosted by
Rcsn01.

OpenSe is beta software. The hosted service is provided for trial and
evaluation only.

## 1. Self-Hosted Instances

If you self-host OpenSe, Rcsn01 does not receive, collect, access, store, or
process the data in your self-hosted instance.

For self-hosted instances, you are responsible for:

- the infrastructure you run OpenSe on;
- your Supabase project or local Supabase instance;
- your users, accounts, authentication, storage, logs, backups, and security;
- complying with any privacy, data protection, employment, customer, or
  industry rules that apply to your use.

Rcsn01 is not responsible for data stored in, processed by, lost from, or
exposed by a self-hosted instance.

## 2. Rcsn01-Hosted Trial/Beta Instances

If you choose to use an OpenSe instance hosted by Rcsn01, the hosted service
stores and processes the data needed to provide the trial service.

This may include:

- account information, such as email address and authentication identifiers;
- organisation, workspace, project, issue, page, message, file, configuration,
  and other application content you create or upload;
- basic technical information produced by Supabase or the application, such as
  authentication events, request information, timestamps, and security logs;
- session information stored in cookies or browser storage to keep you signed
  in and operate the service.

Rcsn01 does not sell your hosted service data, use customer content for
advertising, or use customer content to train artificial intelligence or
machine learning models.

Rcsn01 does not intentionally inspect customer content in the hosted service
except where reasonably necessary to provide support requested by you, maintain
security, investigate abuse, operate the service, or comply with legal
obligations.

## 3. Sources of Data

For the Rcsn01-hosted trial/beta service, data may come from:

- you, when you create an account, create content, upload files, configure
  organisations or workspaces, or contact Rcsn01;
- invited users, workspace members, or organisation members who use the hosted
  service with you;
- the hosted application, when it creates records, timestamps, identifiers,
  settings, logs, and other operational data;
- Supabase, when it provides authentication, database, storage, logging, and
  related backend services;
- your browser or device, when it stores session information or sends ordinary
  request information needed to operate the service.

## 4. How Data Is Used

Rcsn01 uses hosted service data only as needed to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and secure the hosted service;
- authenticate users and keep sessions working;
- store and display the content and files users create or upload;
- support organisations, workspaces, projects, issues, pages, messages,
  settings, and other application features;
- respond to support, privacy, deletion, or security requests;
- detect, investigate, prevent, or respond to abuse, attacks, spam, malware,
  security incidents, or violations of the Terms of Service;
- comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing personal data,
Rcsn01 processes hosted service data as necessary to provide the service,
protect the service and users, comply with legal obligations, and, where
required, with your consent.

## 5. Who Data Is Shared With

Rcsn01 may share hosted service data only as needed with:

- Supabase, which provides authentication, database, storage, logging, and
  related backend infrastructure for the hosted service;
- users, organisation members, workspace members, or invited collaborators to
  whom you or your organisation make content available inside OpenSe;
- service providers or infrastructure providers used to operate, secure, or
  maintain the hosted service;
- legal, safety, or security recipients where reasonably necessary to comply

Rcsn01 does not sell hosted service data and does not share it for targeted

## 6. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

Rcsn01 does not use hosted service data for automated decision-making or

## 7. Supabase

OpenSe uses Supabase for backend services such as authentication, database,
storage, and related infrastructure.

For the Rcsn01-hosted trial/beta service, hosted OpenSe data is stored in the
Supabase environment used to operate that hosted service.

For self-hosted OpenSe, your own Supabase project, local Supabase instance, or
other configured backend is controlled by you, not by Rcsn01.

## 8. Cookies and Browser Storage

OpenSe may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser storage to keep users
signed in, maintain sessions, remember application state, and protect accounts.

These technologies are used for service functionality. OpenSe does not use
advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies unless this policy is
updated to say so.

Do not disable required authentication/session storage if you want the hosted
service to function correctly.

## 9. No Sensitive or Regulated Data

Do not upload or enter sensitive or regulated data into the Rcsn01-hosted
trial/beta service.

- payment card data;
- government identification numbers or identity documents;

- children's data;
- legally privileged, highly confidential, export-controlled, or regulated

The hosted service is a beta trial and is not designed for regulated or
high-risk data.
